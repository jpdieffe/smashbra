Ready, player two?

Create a room and send the invite to your friend. The host runs the original game; the friend controls controller two and receives live video and sound.

  1. Press Start to skip the intro. Choose VS MODE → VS START with the stick and A.
  2. Move your cursor to a fighter and press A to place your token. Set unused slots to CPU or N/A in the original menu.
  3. Press Start when ready, then choose a stage. Knock your rival off the stage!

Use a small stick movement to walk; push it fully to run. Flick the stick and Attack for a smash. Touch controls support simultaneous presses.

Phones, sound & connection help

Rotate sideways. Tap Enable sound if asked. On iPhone you can use Safari’s Share → Add to Home Screen for more space. Keep the host’s game visible; switching apps pauses it. Hosting performance depends on the device.

Room codes identify a room; they are not passwords. Anyone with the code can take the open second-player slot. No microphone or screen-sharing permission is requested.

If the room connects but video does not appear, use Retry. Some Wi-Fi and mobile networks require a TURN relay. Android may also need your browser’s Nearby devices / Local network permission.

Settings stay in this tab and apply to the next room. Both players should use the same PeerServer.
